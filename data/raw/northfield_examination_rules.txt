Northfield University - Examination Rules

Hall tickets are issued through the student portal seven days before the first examination. Students must carry their hall ticket and the university identity card to every examination. Mobile phones, smart watches and any electronic devices are not allowed inside the examination hall. Students must arrive at least 20 minutes before the start of the paper, and entry is not permitted after 30 minutes from the start. Use of unfair means leads to cancellation of the paper and may lead to suspension for one or more semesters, as decided by the Examination Disciplinary Committee. A student who misses an examination because of a medical emergency may apply for a make-up examination within five working days with a medical certificate. Re-evaluation of an answer script can be requested within ten days of result declaration by paying 1000 rupees per paper. The re-evaluated marks replace the original marks even if they are lower.
