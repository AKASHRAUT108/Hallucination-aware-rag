Northfield University - Attendance Policy

Students must maintain at least 75 percent attendance in every course to be eligible to sit the end-semester examination. Attendance is recorded at each lecture and laboratory session by the course instructor. A student whose attendance falls between 65 and 74 percent may apply for condonation by submitting a written request to the Head of Department together with supporting documents such as a medical certificate. Condonation can be granted at most twice during the full degree programme and carries a fee of 500 rupees per course. Students below 65 percent attendance are not eligible for condonation and must repeat the course in the next academic year. Attendance for approved university activities such as sports events counts as present when the activity is authorised in writing by the Dean of Student Affairs. Attendance records are published on the student portal at the end of every month.
