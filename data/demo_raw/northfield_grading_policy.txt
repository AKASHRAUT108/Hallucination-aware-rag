Northfield University - Grading Policy

The university uses a ten point grading scale. Grades are awarded as O (10 points) for 90 percent and above, A+ (9 points) for 80 to 89 percent, A (8 points) for 70 to 79 percent, B+ (7 points) for 60 to 69 percent, B (6 points) for 50 to 59 percent, and C (5 points) for 40 to 49 percent. A mark below 40 percent is a fail and is recorded as grade F with zero points. The Semester Grade Point Average is the credit-weighted average of grade points earned in a semester. The Cumulative Grade Point Average is calculated over all completed semesters. A minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average of 5.0 is required to progress to the next academic year. Internal assessment contributes 40 percent of the final marks and the end-semester examination contributes 60 percent. A student must score at least 40 percent in the end-semester examination alone to pass a course.
