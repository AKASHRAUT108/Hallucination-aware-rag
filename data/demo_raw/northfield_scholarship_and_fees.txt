Northfield University - Scholarships and Fees

The Merit Scholarship is awarded to students who achieve a Cumulative Grade Point Average of 9.0 or higher in the previous academic year. It covers 50 percent of the annual tuition fee. The Need-Based Grant is available to students whose annual family income is below 4 lakh rupees and requires an income certificate issued within the last six months. A student can hold only one university scholarship at a time. Tuition fees are paid in two instalments, the first before the start of the odd semester and the second before the start of the even semester. A late fee of 200 rupees per week is charged for delayed payment, up to a maximum of 2000 rupees. Hostel fees are billed separately from tuition fees. Scholarship renewal is reviewed every year and a student whose Cumulative Grade Point Average drops below the required level loses the scholarship for the following year.
